## 4. App notifications (MyClinicSoft → clinics)

Send from **Platform → Notify** (`/platform/notifications`). Limits: title up to 200 characters, message up to 2,000, and the link must be a path inside the app (it can't be the Play Store link). Phones cut off push text after about 40 characters of title and 100 of message, so the first line has to make sense on its own.

### N-1 · Clinic admins · eMedRoute is live

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | Clinic admins |
| Importance | Normal (it still gets pushed, because it goes to admins) |
| Link | `/medical-reps` |
| Title | Reps can now book your rep hours |
| Message | Medical reps can now book a visit inside your clinic's rep hours with eMedRoute, the new app for reps on Google Play. No more walk-ins in the middle of a consult. Rep visits are always free for your clinic. Open Medical Reps to set your rep hours and see who has booked. |

### N-2 · All clinic staff · Heads-up for the front desk

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | All clinic staff |
| Importance | Normal (shows in the app only, no push) |
| Link | `/medical-reps` |
| Title | Rep visits now come booked |
| Message | Medical reps can now book a slot in your clinic's rep hours through the eMedRoute app. Booked visits show under Medical Reps, so you'll know who is coming and when. If a rep asks where to book, tell them to search "eMedRoute" on Google Play. |

### N-3 · Clinic admins · Sponsorship (send 1–2 weeks after N-1)
