# MyClinicSoft Business Profile

## Company

**Product:** MyClinicSoft  
**Built by:** DevCom Digital  
**Industry:** Healthcare technology / clinic practice management  
**Primary market:** Clinics and medical practices in the Philippines

## Overview

MyClinicSoft is an integrated clinic management platform built by DevCom Digital to help Philippine clinics coordinate their day-to-day operations in one place. It brings patient records, scheduling, clinical documentation, billing, inventory, and patient communication together so clinic teams can spend less time managing disconnected administrative tasks.

The platform is designed for the people who keep a clinic running: doctors and clinical staff, practice owners and administrators, reception and front-desk teams, billing staff, and inventory managers. Its connected patient-facing companion, eMCR (Electronic MyClinic Record), gives patients tools for booking and accessing selected clinic information.

## The Need We Address

Clinic workflows often span separate systems and manual processes. This can make it harder for staff to coordinate appointments, maintain current records, manage patient flow, complete documentation, and keep operational information in sync.

MyClinicSoft brings these workflows into a shared platform, helping clinic teams manage the patient journey from registration and booking through visits, prescriptions, results, and billing.

## Products

### MyClinicSoft

The clinic-facing platform for managing practice operations and clinical workflows. Core capabilities include:

- Patient profiles, medical histories, alerts, and document attachments
- Doctor and staff profiles, schedules, roles, and permissions
- Appointment calendars, online booking, reminders, waitlists, and recurring visits
- Patient check-in, QR-based queue management, and queue displays
- Visit notes, vitals, diagnoses, treatment plans, and medical certificates
- E-prescriptions, medication history, and interaction checks
- Lab requests, results, and patient notifications
- Referrals and patient follow-up workflows
- Invoices, payment tracking, receipts, and support for PhilHealth and HMO claims
- Medicine and supply inventory, including stock and expiry tracking
- Operational reports, audit logs, and administrative tools

### eMCR

The Electronic MyClinic Record patient app connects patients with participating clinics. Its documented capabilities include appointment booking, live queue updates, and access to available results and prescriptions.

## Who It Serves

MyClinicSoft is intended for:

- Independent clinics and growing medical practices
- Multi-doctor practices coordinating staff and patient workflows
- Clinic administrators and owners overseeing operations
- Front-desk teams managing bookings, check-ins, and queues
- Clinical staff documenting visits and coordinating care
- Finance and billing staff handling invoices and claims
- Inventory teams tracking medicines and supplies
- Patients using connected self-service features through eMCR

## Customer Value

By connecting clinic workflows in one system, MyClinicSoft aims to help practices:

- Reduce reliance on disconnected tools and repetitive administrative work
- Keep patient, appointment, clinical, and billing information together
- Coordinate front-desk, clinical, and administrative work more consistently
- Give patients convenient digital options for booking and accessing information
- Support traceability through role-based access and audit logging

These are product goals, not guaranteed clinical or financial outcomes; results depend on each clinic's workflows and implementation.

## Privacy and Security

MyClinicSoft is designed for the Philippine healthcare context, with features including role-based access controls, two-factor sign-in, audit logging, and data-handling practices aligned with the project's stated focus on the Philippine Data Privacy Act (PH DPA). Clinics remain responsible for their own lawful data processing, configuration, access management, and operational compliance.

The platform also supports backups and uses configurable integrations for services such as document storage, SMS, and email. Availability and behavior of third-party services depend on their configuration and terms.

## Plans and Getting Started

The product website describes a 14-day free trial and monthly or yearly subscription plans for different clinic sizes. Plan features and limits vary; current pricing and terms should be confirmed through the official MyClinicSoft website.
