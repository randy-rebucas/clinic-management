# Customer FAQ

## Summary

The Customer FAQ answers the questions clinics, patients and visitors ask most about MyClinicSoft and the eMCR (Electronic MyClinic Record) patient app. It is for the support team and sales, and every answer is safe to give to a customer. The answers come from the public website (FAQ, pricing, compare, security, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, both updated 2026-10-22) and the eMCR app documentation. Prices and plan limits are not repeated here; they are in "Products and Services Catalogue". Where the public pages and the product disagree, the answer carries a [TO CONFIRM] marker and is listed under Open questions.

## What is MyClinicSoft?

MyClinicSoft is a clinic management system built by DevCom Digital, a business registered in the Philippines. It helps clinics manage patients, appointments, queues, visits, prescriptions, lab results, billing with PhilHealth and HMO claims, and inventory in one platform. Patients connect to their clinic through the eMCR app.

## Do I need technical knowledge to use MyClinicSoft?

MyClinicSoft does not need technical knowledge: it is built for healthcare professionals, not IT experts. DevCom Digital provides documentation and support to help a clinic team get started.

## Is MyClinicSoft cloud-based?

MyClinicSoft is cloud-based. Clinics use it from anywhere with an internet connection, with no installation or server maintenance.

## Can I try MyClinicSoft before paying?

Every clinic can try MyClinicSoft with a 14-day free trial [TO CONFIRM: trial length (contradictions 12 and 42)]. No credit card is needed to start. The Free trial includes the core features, with the limits shown in the "Products and Services Catalogue" [TO CONFIRM: what the trial includes (contradiction 3)].

## What happens when the free trial ends?

When the free trial ends, a clinic chooses a paid plan to keep going, and everything set up during the trial stays as it is. MyClinicSoft reminds the clinic 3 days and 1 day before the trial ends. If the clinic does not subscribe, the rules in "What happens if my subscription is not renewed?" apply.

## How much does MyClinicSoft cost?

MyClinicSoft plan prices, monthly and yearly, are listed in the "Products and Services Catalogue" and on the pricing page at www.myclinicsoft.solutions. Prices are in Philippine pesos. Enterprise pricing is arranged through "Talk to sales".

## Are there setup fees or hidden fees?

MyClinicSoft has no setup fee: a clinic pays only the monthly or yearly subscription. The Terms of Service say clinics are responsible for applicable taxes [TO CONFIRM: whether published prices include VAT (contradiction 51)].

## What payment methods do you accept?

MyClinicSoft subscriptions are paid in Philippine pesos through PayMongo, which accepts GCash, Maya, credit and debit cards, GrabPay and QR Ph. Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice. Subscriptions are billed in advance, monthly or yearly.

## Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yearly billing for MyClinicSoft costs the price of 10 months, so a clinic gets two months free compared with paying monthly [TO CONFIRM: yearly discount wording (contradiction 9)]. The yearly prices are in the "Products and Services Catalogue".

## Can I change my plan later?

Clinics can switch plans from the clinic's subscription page. How the change is charged and when it takes effect is [TO CONFIRM: how plan changes are charged (contradiction 1)].

## Do you offer refunds?

MyClinicSoft payments are final and non-refundable, including for unused time in a billing period, downgrades and accounts closed early, except where Philippine law requires otherwise [TO CONFIRM: refund policy (contradiction 11)]. The free trial is there so a clinic can make sure MyClinicSoft fits before paying.

## What happens if my subscription is not renewed?

When a MyClinicSoft subscription is not renewed, the clinic's account becomes read-only for a 7-day grace period, so the clinic can still view and export its records. After that, access is suspended until the clinic subscribes again. The clinic's data is not deleted because a payment was missed.

## Can prices change?

DevCom Digital may change MyClinicSoft prices with at least 30 days' notice, and a change applies from the clinic's next billing period. Material changes to the Terms of Service are announced to clinic administrators at least 15 days before they take effect.

## What features are included in the Basic plan?

The Basic plan includes the core features: patient management, appointment scheduling, visit management, prescriptions, lab results, billing, inventory and basic reporting. It suits small clinics. The full comparison of plans, including the doctor and user limits, is in the "Products and Services Catalogue".

## Can I customise MyClinicSoft or connect my own systems?

The Professional and Enterprise plans add custom reports, a REST API and signed webhooks for a clinic's own integrations. Enterprise also includes custom integrations built with the DevCom Digital team.

## Does MyClinicSoft support multiple locations?

Each clinic location runs as its own MyClinicSoft account today. Managing several locations from one account is on the roadmap, and clinics that need it can contact the team [TO CONFIRM: multi-location support (contradiction 5)].

## Does MyClinicSoft handle PhilHealth and HMO claims?

Every MyClinicSoft plan includes billing with PhilHealth and HMO claims, tracked per visit together with invoices and payments.

## Does MyClinicSoft integrate with lab systems?

MyClinicSoft supports third-party lab integrations. API access for a clinic's own integrations is covered in "Can I customise MyClinicSoft or connect my own systems?".

## Can patients book appointments online?

Patients can book online at any time through the clinic's booking page or the eMCR app, based on the doctors' schedules and the clinic's hours. The patient chooses the clinic, a doctor and an open time, and the clinic confirms the request [TO CONFIRM: whether confirmations are sent by SMS before SMS goes live (contradiction 50)].

## Is MyClinicSoft built for the Data Privacy Act?

MyClinicSoft is built for the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), with role-based access controls, two-factor sign-in and audit logs of access to patient records. The clinic remains the personal information controller for the patient data it enters and is responsible for its own lawful processing.

## How is patient data protected?

MyClinicSoft encrypts all traffic with TLS, and the database is encrypted at rest [TO CONFIRM: encryption at rest (contradiction 29)]. Sign-in secrets and integration keys get an extra layer of AES-256-GCM encryption. Role-based access controls limit who sees what, every clinic's data is kept separate, staff can turn on authenticator-app codes, and sign-ins, record access and changes are logged.

## Where is my data stored?

MyClinicSoft data is stored on cloud infrastructure run by service providers listed in the Privacy Policy. Some providers are based outside the Philippines, mainly in the United States, and DevCom Digital chooses providers that protect data to a standard comparable to the Data Privacy Act. Clinic administrators can also take backups of their clinic's data from inside the app at any time [TO CONFIRM: backup arrangements for the service (contradiction 30)].

## Can I export my data?

Clinics can export their data while the account is active, and during the read-only grace period after a subscription ends [TO CONFIRM: whether the Free trial includes data export (contradiction 4)].

## Who owns the data my clinic enters?

The clinic owns the data it enters in MyClinicSoft. DevCom Digital processes it only to provide the service, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## Is patient data used for marketing or AI training?

Clinic patient data in MyClinicSoft is processed on the clinic's instructions. DevCom Digital does not use it for its own marketing, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models.

## Can I rely on AI visit notes?

AI visit notes are drafts that may be incomplete or wrong, and a licensed professional must review them before they are saved, signed or relied on. MyClinicSoft is a records and workflow tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. AI visit notes are available on the plans shown in the "Products and Services Catalogue" and run only when a clinic turns the feature on.

## What kind of support do you offer?

All MyClinicSoft plans include email support; the support level for each plan, including Enterprise contract terms, is in the "Products and Services Catalogue" [TO CONFIRM: support level per plan (contradiction 6)]. Phone and chat support are not offered.

## What are your support hours?

MyClinicSoft support hours are Monday–Friday 9:00 AM–6:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM–4:00 PM, and closed on Sunday [TO CONFIRM: support hours are Asia/Manila time]. Enterprise support hours are agreed in the clinic's contract.

## How quickly do you respond to support requests?

The MyClinicSoft support team answers during support hours, in the order messages arrive, with Professional and Enterprise requests handled first. No fixed response time is published [TO CONFIRM: response targets].

## How do I contact support?

Clinic administrators get the fastest help by signing in and opening a support request in Admin → Support; replies come in the app and by email, and other staff ask their clinic administrator. Anyone else, including people who cannot sign in, can write to support@myclinicsoft.solutions or use the contact form on the website.

## Do you provide training?

DevCom Digital provides documentation and guides for every MyClinicSoft module, and Enterprise customers receive personalised training sessions [TO CONFIRM: what the Enterprise training includes].

## How long does it take to set up MyClinicSoft?

A clinic can start using MyClinicSoft right after signing up, and basic setup of the clinic, doctors and schedules takes a few minutes. How long a full rollout takes depends mostly on how many existing records the clinic brings over.

## Can I import data from my current system?

Clinics can send their existing records, for example as CSV or Excel exports, and the DevCom Digital team helps bring them over into MyClinicSoft. Larger migrations from other systems are handled together with the team.

## What happens to my data if I cancel?

A clinic can export its data before cancelling MyClinicSoft. After the account is closed, a clinic administrator can ask in writing for the clinic's data to be deleted, and DevCom Digital deletes it within 30 days of the request, except for anything the law requires it to keep.

## How do I report a security problem?

Security problems with MyClinicSoft are reported privately by email to support@myclinicsoft.solutions with the subject "Security report". Suspected unauthorised access to a clinic's accounts is reported promptly to legal@myclinicsoft.solutions, as the Terms of Service require.

## What is the eMCR app?

eMCR (Electronic MyClinic Record) is the patient app for MyClinicSoft clinics. Patients use it to book, reschedule and cancel visits, follow their place in the queue live, check in, and see lab results, prescriptions, bills and documents from their clinic, with one sign-in across partner clinics. The app is available in English and Filipino.

## Is eMCR available on iPhone?

eMCR is available now on Android through Google Play. The iOS version is coming soon [TO CONFIRM: iOS release date (contradiction 28)].

## Does eMCR cost anything?

Clinics do not pay for eMCR. Some eMCR features may need activation with a voucher bought for cash at a participating clinic; each voucher activates the account for one year, and a renewal adds a year to the current expiry. The voucher price is in the "Products and Services Catalogue" [TO CONFIRM: eMCR fee disclosure (contradiction 7)]. Without activation, a patient can always view a basic summary of their own records. Vouchers are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## How do patients sign in to eMCR?

Patients sign in to eMCR with their mobile number and password, a one-time code sent by text, or the login QR code or invite from their clinic [TO CONFIRM: text sign-in codes before SMS goes live (contradiction 50)]. A clinic invite works once and lasts 14 days; a patient whose invite has expired asks the clinic for a new one.

## Who do I contact about my medical records?

A patient's clinic manages their medical records, so the patient contacts the clinic first for corrections or copies. For problems with the eMCR app itself, the patient contacts the MyClinicSoft support team at support@myclinicsoft.solutions.

## Can I manage my family's records in eMCR?

A parent or guardian can add a child as a dependant in eMCR and switch between family profiles. eMCR accounts are held by adults, and a dependant's access passes to them when they turn 18.

## Is my health information safe on my phone?

eMCR keeps sign-in tokens in the phone's secure storage, and records saved for offline viewing are encrypted on the device. While a patient is signed in, screenshots are blocked and the app-switcher preview is hidden. Patients can also turn on App Lock, which asks for a fingerprint, face or screen lock when the app opens.

## How do I stop text messages from my clinic?

A patient stops text messages from a clinic through the link on promotional texts, in the eMCR notification settings, or by asking the clinic. Sign-in codes are always sent [TO CONFIRM: SMS go-live date (contradiction 50)].

## Is there an app for medical representatives?

A separate MyClinicSoft Rep app for medical representatives is coming soon, and rep registration on the website is paused while it is built [TO CONFIRM: Rep app release date (contradiction 17)]. Clinics a representative already works with can add them today.
